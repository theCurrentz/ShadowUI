# Forever skins Blizzard action buttons

LibActionButton stays in the tree but is not loaded. Restricted Execute, GetActionInfo, HasAction, Hide/SetAlpha/SetParent/SetPoint/EnableMouse/SetHitRectInsets/SetScript/HookScript/SetFrameLevel on default action hosts, their cooldown frames, and MainMenuBar art are secret or forbidden while this addon is tainted. `pcall` still pops the ADDON_ACTION_FORBIDDEN dialog. Hide, SetScript, and SetAlpha on Store, Help, Encounter Journal, Collections, Achievement, extra bag slots, and the keyring do the same. Those buttons stay on the Blizzard bars. Hide and EnableMouse on MinimapCluster, and Hide/SetPoint/SetClipsChildren on MicroMenuContainer, do the same. Cooldown times and GetTexture file IDs are secret; chrome does not compare them. ShadowUI does not replace `ActionButton_UpdateCooldown` and does not write keys (`icon`, `HotKey`, `Count`) on default action hosts. Blizzard `Update` reads both; that taint stopped ACTIONBAR_SHOWGRID from showing empty slots, so a drop on an empty slot clicked through to the world. Outer Edge backdrop math skips a secret width, which loot reports on the chrome frame. Live ActionButtons stay Blizzard hosts. Chrome is a Darken rim (a vertex colour on the Blizzard slot and pressed rim) and an Outer Edge. The icon, IconMask, cooldowns, hover and checked glows, Flash, equipped Border, and spell alerts stay native. An icon crop, a mask strip, or a fill would cut into Blizzard proc, cast, and cooldown feedback, which is sized to the native icon. Button place stays with the Blizzard bar. Gryphon and bar art keep Blizzard show state; Darken tints them. The Micro Bar and the Bag Bar stay with Blizzard. ShadowUI adds an Outer Edge on those borders ([ADR 0017](0017-micro-and-bag-borders.md)).
