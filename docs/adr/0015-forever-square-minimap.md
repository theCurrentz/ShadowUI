# Forever square minimap skins the Blizzard cluster

The Classic Era square map reparented `Minimap` into a ShadowUI holder and hid GameTimeFrame, zoom, and cluster header widgets. Forever uses the retail cluster: header Zone Text, Time, calendar, mail, and tracking stay. ShadowUI squares the map mask, hides the compass ring and Forever `DielFrame` (day/night dial over the map face), and puts a Darken fade square plus Outer Edge behind the map. Left-click pings the full square face. It does not Hide or EnableMouse `MinimapCluster`. Map-edge icons still sit on the SexyMap square path.

## Considered Options

- Keep the Classic Era holder that relocates Zone Text and Time — rejected; that strips Forever header widgets.
- Hide GameTimeFrame as day/night art — rejected; on Forever it is the calendar.

Status: accepted.
